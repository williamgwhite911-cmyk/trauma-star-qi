Signature & Authorization — Billing Requirement

Medicare will not pay a claim without the beneficiary's signature authorizing the claim and the release of information. When the patient cannot sign and no authorized representative is available, the record must carry why the patient could not sign and a contemporaneous attestation from the receiving facility or another provider employee. This section is scanned from the chart; correct it here if the scan is wrong.
